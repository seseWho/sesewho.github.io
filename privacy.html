Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Herni is a personal application used only by its owner.

  • It requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.
  • It uses it only to send notification emails (trading activity) from the owner's account.
  • It does not read, store, analyse or share any email, contact or other Google user data.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own machines and is never transmitted to third parties.
  • Herni's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access at any time: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
